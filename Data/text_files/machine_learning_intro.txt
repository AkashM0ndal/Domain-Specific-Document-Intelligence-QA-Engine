Machine Learning is a subset of artificial Intelligence that enables systems to learn and imporve from experience without being 
explicitly programmed. It focuses on developing computer programs that can access data and use it to learn for themselves.

Types of ML:
1. Supervised Learning: Learning with labeled data 
2. Unsupervised Learning: Finding patterns in unlabeled data
3. reinforcement Learning: Learning through rewars and penalties

Applications include image redcognition, speech processing, and recommendation systems
